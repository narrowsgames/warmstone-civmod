# Warmstone Mod for Civilization VI

This total-conversion mod for Sid Meier's Civilization VI presents the fictional alien world whose name we transcribe as *Warmstone*.


## AI Disclosure

This game project uses AI, but has established boundaries around that usage. Shipped art, copy and documentation, including canon documents accompanying an art request, will all be human authored, and never provided to the AI as input for training or any other purpose except by the author themselves, at their own discretion. For an artist, that means they are the only project member who can decide to provide their art to an AI model. Software will be developed with AI assistance, adding extra audit and testing steps to mitigate the known risks of AI-assisted software engineering.

The project was conceptually and technically developed with extensive AI assistance in the initial phases. The project plan is to continue using AI for inspiration, consistency review, commentary, and as an assistant for software development and testing.
